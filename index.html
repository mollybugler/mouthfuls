Games worth chewing on

A game system that turns whatever’s on your plate into food exploration and fun for the whole family.

Four card decks, dice, and meeples mix and match into four games: a race-to-the-finish board game, a dice game, a challenge game, and a card game. The games are best suited for kids ages 3+ and all flex to fit what’s on your plate, how your kid likes to play, and how much time you’ve got. Forget your mother’s clean-plate club, we believe in meeting kids wherever they are.

Meet Mouthfuls

Kids learn about their world through play, why should food be different?

This idea grew out of shared frustrations at our own dinner tables: food refusals, struggles to keep kids at the table, and yes, even tears shed over sandwiches cut the wrong way. We started to notice that when mealtime felt playful, things changed. Feeding a pretend bunny suddenly got veggies eaten. A food challenge became an incentive to taste something new. Fun was the missing ingredient at our tables and became the foundation of everything we are building at Mouthfuls.
